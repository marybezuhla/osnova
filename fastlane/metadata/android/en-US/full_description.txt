<p>Osnova is the Constitution of Ukraine in Ukrainian and English. The app has no internet permission: everything stays on the phone.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Word for word.</b> The current edition as the Verkhovna Rada publishes it, and the official English translation.</li>
<li><b>Search</b> in both languages, with word forms; "article 24" or "chapter XV" opens the article or chapter at once.</li>
<li><b>Quotes cited as lawyers cite them:</b> the whole article, chosen parts or selected text — "Article 85(2) of the Constitution of Ukraine".</li>
<li><b>Reading aloud</b> offline in Osnova's female voices (downloaded once, 152 and 64 MB) or the phone's voice; it reads with the screen off too.</li>
<li><b>Bookmarks</b> and "Continue reading" from where you stopped.</li>
<li><b>Themes.</b> Light or dark, five day and five night themes or the phone's colours (Android 12+).</li>
</ul>
<p>No ads, accounts or tracking. This is not legal advice. Requires Android 8 or newer.</p>
