Osnova is the Constitution of Ukraine on your phone: the full text of the current edition (from 1 January 2020) word for word as published by the Verkhovna Rada, and the official English translation.

• Reading aloud offline: Osnova's female voices (Ukrainian with the right stresses, and English) are downloaded once (152 MB and 64 MB), or the phone's voice reads. It reads with the screen off too.
• Share a quote: the whole article or chosen parts, cited the way lawyers do ("Article 85, paragraph 2").
• Search in both languages, with word forms; an article number opens the article at once.
• Bookmarks and "Continue reading" from where you stopped.
• Switch the language of the text on any article without losing your place.
• Light and dark themes with Ukrainian names, or the phone's colours.

No internet, ads, accounts or tracking. Its only permission lets it read aloud with the screen off.

Requires Android 12 or newer.

This is not legal advice. The official text: zakon.rada.gov.ua.

The code was written with AI (Claude, Anthropic), directed and tested by the author.
